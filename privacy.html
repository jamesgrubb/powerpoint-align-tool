Slide Tools privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026. Slide Tools is provided by James Grubb Ltd (company number 11924116), 12 Wharton Road, Bromley, Kent, BR1 3LF, United Kingdom.

Presentation data

Slide Tools reads selected objects’ positions, sizes and rotation, and reads slide dimensions when needed to align an object to the slide. It changes object positions inside PowerPoint. It requests ReadWriteDocument permission for these operations. The application does not upload presentation contents to our server, create app accounts, run analytics or advertising, or store presentation contents in a database.

Loading the add-in

Your device downloads application files from Railway, our hosting provider. Network requests expose ordinary connection information, including IP address and requested resource, to the hosting provider. Our application server does not create its own request log or use tracking cookies. Hosting providers may process service and network metadata under their own policies. Microsoft supplies Office.js, the Office runtime and shortcut preferences under its policies.

We keep it only as long as necessary for those purposes and any applicable legal obligations. Please do not send confidential presentations or unnecessary personal information.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, or object to processing. Contact JamesGrubb@MakingThingsBetterLtd.onmicrosoft.com. Sharing and changes

We do not sell personal information. Service providers used for hosting and correspondence may process information to deliver those services; processing may occur outside your country.
